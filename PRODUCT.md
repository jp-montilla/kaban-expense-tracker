# Kaban

## Register
product

## Purpose
Household money tracker for one person who needs cash left this cutoff, a daily log, bills, loans, and Z400 service in one place.

## Users
The household owner, usually on a laptop or phone after payday or after spending. Primary task on any screen: see the number that matters, then log or edit without hunting through a wall of copy.

## Personality
Calm, direct, money-serious.

## Principles
- Cash left is the hero. Everything else supports it.
- Forms open in a dialog. Lists stay lists.
- One line of helper text, or none.
- Familiar product UI over a paper-ledger costume.

## Money rules
- Living pool is ₱17,000 a month. Gas, grocery, wants, and the rest of the log share that one number.
- Reset bills plus loans plus living is the month plan (₱42,510).
- Each month’s pay is last month-end salary plus this month’s 15 salary.
- October 2026 is Sep 30 ₱30,000 plus Oct 15 ₱55,000 (₱85,000). November 2026 is Oct 30/31 ₱55,000 plus Nov 15 ₱55,000 (₱110,000).
- Add to savings comes out of cash left. Starting pile does not.
- Cash is a locked payment option. Wallets, credit, and bank/debit are groups you add under. Log items and bills can be paid via credit: pick SPay, LazPay, or a card, then months and monthly pay. Those installments land on Bills as pay-only dues. SPay is due on the 15th, LazPay on the 16th.

## Anti-references
Cream parchment backgrounds, serif display numbers, uppercase kickers on every block, long moss essays under every tile, inline create/edit forms stacked on the page.
